# Explainer video — narration

The voiceover of [HPC-Career-Demo.mp4](HPC-Career-Demo.mp4) (2:21, 1080p, captioned). Voice: macOS system voice "Tara" (English, India).

**0:00 · intro** — Every student deserves a career conversation. This is HPC Career: a Holistic Progress Card with career guidance built in.

**0:09 · problem** — India has about twenty-four point eight crore school students. In one survey of ten thousand students, ninety-three percent knew only seven career options, out of roughly two hundred and fifty. And among low-income students in Classes nine to twelve, fewer than one in ten had ever received career guidance.

**0:26 · gap** — The stream decision comes at fifteen, and it is mostly made on advice from home. The new Holistic Progress Card describes the whole child, but it stops short of the question every Class ten student faces: what next?

**0:40 · solution** — HPC Career adds a Career tab to the progress card: a ten-minute test, a report written for the student, real careers with real routes, and a counsellor to ask.

**0:50 · demo** — Here is Asha. She signs in with the code her school gave her, opens the Career tab, and starts the test. Fifty-six quick questions, one per screen: first what she would enjoy doing, then how she usually is. When she finishes, the server scores her answers, and her report is ready.

**1:21 · report** — Asha is a Thinker and a Helper. Her report explains what that means, describes her personality as strengths, and lists six careers that fit, each with the route from Class ten. It prints, so her family can talk it through.

**1:34 · explore** — She can explore thirty-six careers, sorted by how well they fit, and ask the counsellor about streams, subjects and exams.

**1:42 · teacher** — Her teacher sees the whole class: who has finished, where their interests lie, and each student's code. A starting point for every one-to-one and parent meeting.

**1:52 · honest** — It is open source and simple to run, and honest about its limits: the report calls itself a starting point, not a verdict. Passwords are hashed, and only the student and their teacher see a result.

**2:02 · next** — Next: the counsellor is becoming an AI agent, built in AgentFoundry, that reads the student's result and finds careers. Then we want to pilot it with a partner school, and add Hindi and more Indian languages.

**2:15 · close** — Every student deserves a career conversation. HPC Career puts one in their pocket.

Sources for the figures: UDISE+ 2023-24 (Ministry of Education); Mindler survey of 10,000 students aged 14–21 (2019); Bharat Career Aspiration Report 2024 (UNICEF YuWaah & iDreamCareer, 4,968 Class 9–12 students from low-income families in 25 states).
