# The pilot session: a 40-minute plan for the teacher

One class period, in the ICT lab or on school phones, with the class teacher
present. Each student needs a device to themselves for the 40 minutes; if there
aren't enough, run two groups one after the other.

## The day before

- [ ] Signed consent slips are back. Only students with one take part; the
      others do the alternative activity named in the note.
- [ ] Slips are printed and cut, one per student, and one for the teacher
      (code T01).
- [ ] The lab is booked and the devices are charged.
- [ ] On one lab machine, open the pilot link and sign in with the teacher's
      slip. If the school network blocks it, try a phone hotspot.
- [ ] A copy of the observation sheet (below) is printed.

## The session

| Time | What happens | What the teacher does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 min | **Welcome.** Read the script below. Hand out the slips. | Make sure every student knows they can stop at any time. |
| 5–8 min | **Sign in.** Open the link on the slip, choose *Student*, type the code and PIN. | Help anyone stuck. Note how many needed help. |
| 8–11 min | **Step 1: Before you start** (4 questions). | **Don't suggest any careers or give examples aloud** — the first question counts what they already know. If they ask what a word means, explain it, and note the word. |
| 11–25 min | **Step 2: the psychometric test** (56 questions, about 10 minutes). | Remind them there are no right answers and it isn't an exam. Note anyone who finishes in under 4 minutes. |
| 25–32 min | **Read the report** and the careers it suggests. | Walk around; answer questions about the report. This is the part to talk about. |
| 32–36 min | **Step 3: After your report** (6 questions). | Again, don't suggest careers during the first question. |
| 36–40 min | **Wrap up.** Ask them to show the report at home. **Menu → Sign out.** | Check every device is signed out. Collect the slips (keep them in an envelope until the data is deleted). |

Home shows the three steps in order, and each one opens only when the one
before it is done.

## What to say at the start

> Today we're trying a new app that helps you find careers that fit you. It's
> not a test and there are no marks. First, a few questions about what you know
> now; then a questionnaire about what you like doing; then you read your own
> report; then a few questions again.
>
> Answer honestly — there are no right answers. If a question doesn't make
> sense, tap "I don't understand this question". That helps us fix it.
>
> Only you, me and the team who made the app see your answers. You can stop at
> any time, and it won't affect anything.

## Things to know

- **Shared devices:** one student per device at a time. *Menu → Sign out*, or
  closing the browser tab, signs them out and clears what they did on that
  device.
- **Lost slip:** the teacher can reissue a PIN with
  `npm run pilot:accounts -- … --reset` (see the [kit](README.md)); for the day,
  pair the student with a spare slip if one was printed.
- **Locked out:** five wrong PINs lock an account for 15 minutes.
- **Anything worrying:** if anything a student says or writes worries you,
  follow the school's usual safeguarding steps.
